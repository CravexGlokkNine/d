Omnix Client Keybinds
=====================

RIGHT SHIFT
  Open or close the Omnix overlay menu.

F8
  Open the quick profile selector for local visual/HUD/performance presets.

Notes
-----
- Keybinds are reserved for compliant client preferences only.
